Made for

Find your next song, fast.

Discover opens on albums on the rise, with Trending, Billboard, Latest and Albums charts below. Tap anything and listen.

Your whole library, one place.

Liked songs, playlists with mood tags, followed artists and offline downloads, plus the files already on your phone.

Name that song, right in the app.

Hear something out loud? Tap the Shazam button, hold your phone near the music, and gamut tells you what is playing.

A player that wears the cover.

Colors come from the artwork. Synced lyrics light up line by line under the controls.

Mod any song.

Nightcore, Daycore, Slowed + Reverb, Lo-Fi, or dial speed, pitch and bass yourself. Pitch can follow speed, tape style, or stay in key.

A 10-band parametric EQ.

Tune each band from 60 Hz to 14 kHz, pick a preset, or reshape frequency and Q. Automatic headroom lowers the level to match the EQ.

Share a card, not just a link.

Send the song as an image with its link, or just the URL, in the color you like.

Favorites, fanned out.

Shuffle, download or play your liked songs from a list that tilts away from the track you are on.

Still have questions?

  • A full music streaming app for Android. Search and play songs, build playlists, follow artists, download for offline and sync across devices. It is free, open source and has no subscription.

  • Yes. Streaming, downloads and every feature are free. gamut is open source under the GNU AGPLv3 license, so you can read the code, change it and share it, as long as your changes stay open under the same license.

    View the source

  • No. You can search, stream and build a library without signing up. Sign in with Google only if you want your favorites, playlists and followed artists to sync across devices.

  • gamut searches and streams from YouTube Music, and also plays the files already on your phone, so everything lives in one library.

  • Yes. Songs you download in gamut play with no connection at all, and the files on your phone always play.

  • It saves your favorite songs, playlists and the artists you follow, so they come with you to a new phone. That is all gamut stores. Details are in the privacy policy.

    Read the privacy policy

  • gamut is an Android app. It runs on Android phones and tablets, and also supports Android Auto and Android TV. There is no iPhone or desktop version.

  • Download the latest build from the GitHub releases page and open it on your phone. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser for this one time.

    Get the latest build

  • Open an issue on GitHub with what happened and which phone you use. Since the project is open source, pull requests are welcome too.
